# Tesoro Builders
 
**One accountable partner, from first sketch to final walkthrough.**
 
Tesoro Builders is a design-build general contractor serving San Bernardino and Riverside Counties. We coordinate independent architects and engineers, our design partners, so owners, developers, and landlords deal with one party from early budgeting through construction.
 
---
 
## Services
 
- General contracting and general construction
- Home construction, including custom homes
- Remodeling
- ADU construction
- Commercial projects
- Commercial tenant improvements
- Real estate development
Within these services we coordinate pre-construction and budgeting, permits and entitlements, and design-build coordination.
 
## Who we work with
 
Developers, municipal officials, owners of high-value residential lots, commercial landlords, and owner-users and investors who want execution certainty.
 
## Service area
 
San Bernardino and Riverside Counties.
